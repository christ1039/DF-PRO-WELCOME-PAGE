04 · Choose Your Environment

Train where you’re most likely to stay consistent.

You can make meaningful progress at home or in a gym. Your environment does not determine whether you succeed. Consistency does.

Option 01

Home

Best if you value convenience or are still building gym confidence.

  • Dumbbells
  • Resistance bands
  • Exercise mat
  • Sturdy bench or elevated surface
Option 02

Gym

Best if you want access to heavier resistance, machines, cables, barbells, and a wider exercise selection.

Option 03

Both

Train at the gym when possible and use home workouts when life gets busy.

If You Miss a Day

Do not restart your entire week.

Continue with the next planned workout when you can. Move workouts around if needed while still allowing adequate recovery.

Schedule Flexibility

You do not have to train Monday through Friday.

The days are a structure, not a prison. Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday can work just as well if that better fits your life.

09 · Track Your Progress

Measure more than the number on the scale.

You cannot improve what you never pay attention to. Depending on your goal, consider tracking the areas below.

Strength

Record weights and repetitions on your main exercises.

Workout Consistency

Notice how many planned sessions you actually complete.

Progress Photos

Optional front, side, and back photos under similar conditions.

Measurements

You may track areas such as your waist and hips.

Body Weight

Optional. Focus on trends instead of individual day-to-day changes.

Daily Movement

Track steps or other general activity when useful.

Habits

Sleep, hydration, meals, and recovery all matter.

How You Feel

Energy, confidence, gym comfort, and performance matter too.

Review your progress approximately every 4 weeks. Avoid judging your body based on a single day.
